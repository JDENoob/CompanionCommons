Our impact

What Pet Families Are Helping Us Understand

Every weekly check-in adds to a growing, real-world picture of pet health — one no single family, or single vet visit, can see alone.

Why this matters

Pet health decisions are still made with a fraction of the picture.

When it comes to human medicine, doctors and researchers can draw on decades of real-world data to understand how conditions actually unfold. Pet health has never had anything remotely close to this — for pet owners, most decisions rely on a single vet visit, memory, a few scattered forum posts, brand-dominated marketing, or guesswork. We're building the missing piece: real, reported data from real dogs, over time.
