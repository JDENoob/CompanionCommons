5. Your Account

A CompanionCommons account is tied to the phone number you use to sign up — one phone number, one account. You can add as many pets as you'd like to a single account; there's no limit.

CompanionCommons does not use passwords. Instead, you access your dashboard and check-ins through unique links, sent to you by text message or email. These links do not expire and are not tied to a separate login — anyone who has one can view your account's information or add a pet to it. Please treat your CompanionCommons links the same way you'd treat a password, and don't share them with anyone you wouldn't want to have that level of access.

If more than one person in your household would like to help track a pet's information, the simplest way is to share your dashboard link with them directly — CompanionCommons does not currently support multiple separate logins for a single pet or account.

If someone signs up using a phone number already associated with an existing account, their new pet is added to that existing account automatically, rather than creating a separate one.

You're responsible for keeping your CompanionCommons links private and for all activity that happens through them. If you lose access to your phone or email, or believe someone else may have obtained your CompanionCommons links, contact us at hello@companioncommons.com and we can help secure your account.
