Privacy Policy

Complete Transparency About Your Pet's Health Data

This is the full, legal version of our data practices. For the plain-language summary, see Trust, Privacy & Independence.

Last updated: September 2, 2026

1. Introduction

CompanionCommons ("CompanionCommons," "we," "us," or "our") operates companioncommons.com and the CompanionCommons pet health logging service (the "Service"). This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and how we share it.

CompanionCommons is controlled and operated from the United States and is not intended to subject us to the laws and jurisdictions of any state, country, or territory other than the United States. The Service is currently offered only to residents of the United States.

By using the Service, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy and in our Terms of Service.

2. What We Collect

When you create an account and log health information for your pet, we collect:

  • Information about you: your first name, email address, phone number, and zip code.
  • Information about your pet: name, breed, age, sex, weight, spayed/neutered status, diet type, whether your pet has pet insurance, medication and supplement category (a defined list of categories, not free-text drug names), the condition being treated (a defined list, with an "other" option), and medication start and stop dates.
  • Pet health check-in information: weekly structured scores across four areas — mobility, energy, appetite, and cognitive/behavioral patterns — reported by you, based on your own observations of your pet.
  • Free-text notes: anything you choose to write in the optional notes fields on your dashboard or in a medication entry.
  • Communication preferences: whether you'd like to receive text messages, email, or both.

We do not collect information passively. CompanionCommons does not use sensors, wearable devices, biometric data, or any form of continuous or automatic tracking. Every piece of pet health information in our system was actively and deliberately entered by you.

3. How We Think About Your Information

We draw a clear line between two kinds of information:

  • Identifying Information — your name, email address, phone number, and zip code. This is information about you, the pet family member using the Service.
  • Pet Health Information — everything about your pet: breed, age, health scores, medication information, and trends over time.

Your pet's health information is not legally classified as "sensitive personal information" under most current privacy frameworks — but we treat it with the same level of care regardless. This is a choice we've made, not a requirement placed on us, because we believe the information you're trusting us with deserves real protection whether or not the law strictly requires it.

4. How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide to:

  • Operate your account and display your pet's check-in history, trends, and dashboard.
  • Send the communications you've asked to receive (see Section 8).
  • Compute the composite scores and trend summaries shown to you.
  • Build the de-identified, aggregated datasets and predictive models described in Section 6.

We never diagnose, interpret, or make health recommendations based on your pet's information. CompanionCommons is not a substitute for veterinary care — see our Terms of Service for more on this.

We never use your pet's health information to target you with advertising or product recommendations. CompanionCommons does not run advertising of any kind, and we never sell or promote any product to you.

5. Free-Text Notes — A Separate Set of Rules

Anything you write in a free-text field — notes on your dashboard, an "other" medication detail, a note on a weekly medication update — is treated differently from every other piece of information you provide.

These notes are never included in any aggregate dataset, licensed dataset, or export of any kind. This isn't just a policy — it's enforced directly in how our systems are built. The parts of our system that prepare information for licensing have no way to read your free-text notes at all.

Your notes remain visible to you, in your own dashboard, for your own use in tracking your pet's journey.

6. How We Share Information

CompanionCommons's business model depends on a clear separation: your pet's health information has real value to researchers and companies working to improve pet health outcomes — your name, email, and phone number never do, and we never share them.

What we share. We license de-identified, aggregated pet health information — and predictive models trained on that information — to organizations working in pet health and wellness, including pharmaceutical companies, research institutions, supplement companies, pet food and nutrition companies, pet insurance companies, pet product manufacturers, and select retailers. This is CompanionCommons's core business model, not an incidental use of your information: it's how we fund a platform that will always be free for pet families to use, with no advertising and no product promotion, ever.

A specific limit on retailers. Retailers who license CompanionCommons information may only receive aggregate, population-level insights — never information tied to you, your pet, or any individual household. Retailers may not use CompanionCommons information to target you with advertising, personalize offers to you, or influence what you individually are shown when you shop. This limit applies to every organization we license information to, not retailers alone — but we're calling it out specifically here because it's the use case people most often (and reasonably) worry about.

What "de-identified and aggregated" actually means. Before any information is shared or licensed, your name, email address, phone number, and zip code are removed entirely. What remains is structured pet health information — scores, categories, dates, trends — that cannot be traced back to you or your pet as individuals. Free-text notes you write are never included in anything we share, under any circumstances; this is enforced directly in how our systems are built, not just a policy we've written down.

What we never do. We never sell your personal information. We never share your name, email, or phone number with any of our licensing partners. We never use your pet's health information to target you with advertising or product recommendations — CompanionCommons does not run advertising of any kind. We never sell or promote any product to you.

Your participation. By logging your pet's health information with CompanionCommons, you're agreeing to let us include your pet's de-identified information in the aggregate datasets and models we license. If you're not comfortable with this, we understand — but CompanionCommons is built around a community of participants working together in hopes of building real knowledge for pet health. If you're unwilling or unable to allow your pet's health information to be licensed, this program can't be offered to you. This licensing model is how we're able to offer the logging experience itself for free, permanently, with no ads and no hidden monetization of your attention.

7. Our De-Identification Commitment

Before any pet health information is included in an aggregate dataset or shared with a licensing partner, we remove your name, email address, phone number, and zip code. Where we maintain or use de-identified information, we will continue to maintain and use it only in a de-identified fashion, and we will not attempt to re-identify it.

8. Communications

If you provide a phone number and opt in to text messages, CompanionCommons will send you check-in reminders and related messages by SMS. Message and data rates may apply. You can stop receiving text messages at any time by replying STOP to any message.

If you provide an email address, CompanionCommons may send you email reminders if you haven't logged a check-in recently. Every one of these emails includes a working unsubscribe link — clicking it will stop these emails going forward.

Opting out of one communication channel does not opt you out of the other. If you'd like to stop all communications, reply STOP to text messages and click unsubscribe in any email, or contact us directly.

9. How Long We Keep Information

De-identified, aggregated pet health information is retained indefinitely, since its value comes from being part of a long-term, growing dataset — unless a specific legal requirement dictates otherwise, or the information is no longer useful to the purposes described in this policy.

Identifying information (your name, email, and phone number) is different: you can request that we delete this information at any time (see Section 11). If you do, your pet's already-contributed de-identified information remains part of the aggregate dataset — it simply can no longer be traced back to you, exactly as it couldn't before your request either.

10. Consent

When you create an account, you're asked to affirmatively consent to CompanionCommons's collection, use, and licensing of your pet's health information as described in this policy. We record the date and time you gave consent, along with the version of this policy in effect at that time.

CompanionCommons's participation and licensing model are offered together, not separately: logging your pet's information means agreeing to let us include it, de-identified, in the datasets we license. See Section 6 for more on why.

11. Your Rights and Choices

You can:

  • Request access to or correction of the identifying information on your account.
  • Request deletion of your identifying information (see Section 9 for what this does and doesn't affect).
  • Opt out of text messages, email, or both (see Section 8).

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at hello@companioncommons.com. We'll respond to and fulfill legitimate requests personally — CompanionCommons does not currently offer self-service tools for viewing or editing your account information directly.

12. Who Can Use CompanionCommons

You must be 18 years of age or older to create an account or provide information about your pet through CompanionCommons.

13. How We Store Your Information

Your information is stored in our database, a secure system built specifically for this purpose. We use industry-standard access controls to limit who and what can reach different parts of our database, and we review our security practices on an ongoing basis.

14. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make a material change, we'll update the effective date at the top of this page and, where appropriate, notify you directly. Your continued use of the Service after a change means you accept the updated policy.

Questions about this policy? Contact us at hello@companioncommons.com.
