Privacy Policy

Complete Transparency About Your Pet's Health Data

This is the full, legal version of our data practices. For the plain-language summary, see Trust, Privacy & Independence.

Last updated: August 16, 2026

Our core promise

Your pet's health data belongs to you. We collect it to help you understand what's normal for your companion and to advance pet health research—but only with your consent, and only in ways you control. We will never sell your personal information for marketing or advertising.

What we collect

When you sign up, we ask for:

  • Your pet's name, breed, age, and gender
  • A baseline health score and activity level
  • Any treatments or supplements they're currently taking
  • Weekly observations about mobility, energy, and changes you notice

What we do NOT collect

  • Veterinary records or medical history (you keep that)
  • Genetic data or biometric scans
  • Payment or financial information
  • Location data or device identifiers
  • Anything beyond what you voluntarily provide

How we protect it

Encryption & storage

All data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Your data is stored with Supabase, a PostgreSQL-based platform with industry-standard security.

Access control

Only authorized Companion Commons staff can view individual user data, and access is logged.

You can delete anytime

You may request deletion of your account and your pet's data at any time. We remove personal data within 30 days of your request. Anonymized data already used in aggregate research may be retained, consistent with the consent you gave at signup.

How we use your data

Three core uses:

Personal health dashboard

See your pet's reported trends over time, and how they compare to similar dogs.

Aggregated research & licensing

De-identified, aggregated data may be shared with research partners and licensed to qualified organizations—including veterinary researchers, pet insurance companies, and pet food or supplement companies studying pet health trends. This is how Companion Commons is funded; see our FAQ for details. Your personal identity is never included.

Product improvement

Understand which questions are most useful and refine the experience over time.

Research & data partnerships

When we share aggregated data with any partner, we commit to:

Consent first

You opt in before your data contributes to any partner-facing research or licensing.

Anonymization

Partners see trends and patterns, never names or personal details.

No selling of identity

Your personal information is never sold. Only de-identified, aggregated data is shared.

Your rights

Access, export, and delete

You can request a copy of your pet's full history at any time, and request deletion of your account and personal data. We comply with GDPR (EU), CCPA (California), and similar regulations, and you have the right to access, correct, delete, and export your data.

Communication controls

Reply STOP to any SMS to opt out immediately, or unsubscribe from emails at any time. Communication is limited to roughly one weekly summary and occasional reminders.

Questions

Email us at hello@companioncommons.com and we'll respond within 48 hours.
