01 — Why we're here

Pet health information is hard to find and harder to trust. We're building a place where pet families can track, understand, and share their companion's health journey. Your participation helps build the knowledge we all need.

My name is John, the founder of Companion Commons. For 30+ years, I've worked in operations, sales and management in a myriad of industries. I also co-founded a CBD brand for dogs and cats. Most recently as SVP Operations for a company that manufactured both human and pet supplements. Pet families deserve information they can trust—not another sales pitch.

We're independent by design. We're not a pet food company, supplement company, insurance company, or marketing company. Our mission is to create the most trusted source of pet health understanding built around transparency, community, and independence.

Independence means we're here to provide a deeper understanding of your pet's health journey, not to represent a product or brand. Your check-ins and baseline submissions help us learn—not sell products.

02 — Your privacy

No. Never. Your personal information is yours alone. We may use aggregated, de-identified data to understand broader health trends, but your identity stays private.

Only what you voluntarily share: your companion's name, age, breed, gender, and answers submitted during the baseline and weekly check-ups. Your notes are stored, but only as a method to populate your dashboard. We do not use notes for any intelligence at this time. You choose what to share.

Your personal information stays private to you. Only aggregated, de-identified companion information is used to understand patterns and trends. You're always in control of what you share.

You can stop anytime. Reply STOP to any SMS, click the unsubscribe link included in any reminder email, or contact us directly using our contact us form. Your journey ends when you want it to.

03 — Your companion's journey

A complete private dashboard that shows you a clearer picture of your companion's health journey over time, a place to keep notes about your pet. Downloadable and printable at any time. Finally, the knowledge that your participation is helping other pet families and advancing our understanding of companion health.

Your initial profile takes less than 3.5 minutes. Weekly check-ins are designed to take about 30 seconds. It's designed to fit into your life, not complicate it.

Yes, there is a button in the baseline form as well as in your dashboard. You can add photos for both and only need to enter your private information once!

04 — The community

Companion Commons is the movement of pet families building a deeper understanding of companion health together. We are just starting this community. As it grows we will introduce ways for all of our pet families to communicate, share experiences and be part of something bigger than just a single user. We are building a movement!

One family's journey is powerful. Thousands of families create a picture. That picture and the knowledge it provides is what changes how we look at pet health understanding, not only for pet owners, but veterinarians, pharmacology, supplements, insurance, research etc.

Create a profile, share your companion's journey weekly and please share and tell other pet families about us. Be part of the movement.

05 — Information & intelligence

To create your companion's Health Journey Profile, provide insights, understand health trends, and improve our tools and offerings — in line with the permissions outlined in our Privacy Policy.

We combine data from many pets to see patterns without revealing who anyone is. For example: “Dogs with these characteristics often show these trends”—not “John's dog has this condition.”

Through aggregated insights, research, and enterprise licensing. The consumer experience is free forever. We believe transparency about business model builds trust.
