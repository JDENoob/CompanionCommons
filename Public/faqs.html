01 — Why we're here

Pet health information is hard to find and harder to trust. We're building a place where pet families can track, understand, and share their companion's health journey. Your participation helps build the knowledge we all need.

I'm John, founder of Companion Commons. For 30+ years, I've worked in operations and management. I've also co-founded a CBD brand for pets. Pet families deserve information they can trust—not another sales pitch.

We're independent by design. We're not a pet food company, supplement company, insurance company, or marketing company. Our mission is to create the most trusted source of pet health understanding built around transparency, community, and independence.

Independence means we're here to provide a deeper understanding of your pet's health journey, not to represent a product or brand. Your data helps us learn—not sell.

02 — Your privacy

No. Never. Your personal information is yours alone. We may use aggregated, de-identified data to understand broader health trends, but your identity stays private.

Only what you voluntarily share: your companion's name, age, breed, gender, health scale, activity level, and health updates. Nothing more. You choose what to share.

Your personal data stays private to you. Only aggregated, de-identified data is used to understand patterns and trends. You're always in control of what you share.

You can stop anytime. Reply STOP to any SMS, unsubscribe from emails, or contact us directly. Your journey ends when you want it to.

03 — Your companion's journey

A clearer picture of your companion's health journey over time, plus the knowledge that your participation is helping other pet families and advancing our understanding of companion health.

Your initial profile takes less than 3 minutes. Weekly check-ins take about 30 seconds. It's designed to fit into your life, not complicate it.

Yes. You can create profiles for both dogs and cats. Each companion gets their own health journey.

04 — The community

Companion Commons is the movement of pet families building a deeper understanding of companion health together. It's about community, transparency, and independence.

One family's journey is powerful. Thousands of families create a picture. That picture—that knowledge—helps veterinarians, researchers, and pet families everywhere.

Create a profile, share your companion's journey, and tell other pet families about us. Be part of the movement.
