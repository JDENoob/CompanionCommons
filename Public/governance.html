🔒 Our Core Promise

Your pet's health data belongs to you. We collect it to help you understand what works best for your companion and to advance pet health research—but only with your explicit consent, and only in ways you control.

What We Collect

When you sign up, we ask for:

  • Your pet's name, breed, age, and gender
  • Current health score and activity level
  • Any treatments or supplements they're taking
  • Weekly observations about mobility, energy, and changes

What We Do NOT Collect

  • Veterinary records or medical history (you keep that)
  • Genetic data or biometric scans
  • Payment or financial information
  • Location data or device identifiers
  • Anything beyond what you voluntarily provide

🔐 How We Protect It

Encryption & Storage

All data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Your database is managed by Supabase, a PostgreSQL-based platform with industry-standard security.

Access Control

Only authorized CompanionCommons staff can view individual user data. We use role-based access controls and audit logging to track every access.

No Selling, No Brokers

We will never sell your data. We may partner with researchers or pet health companies, but only when it advances pet health—and you'll know exactly who, when, and why.

You Can Delete Anytime

Want to leave? You can request deletion of your pet's data at any time. We'll remove it within 30 days, and it won't be used in any new research.

⚖️ Governance & Accountability

Our Founding Commitment

CompanionCommons is built on a simple principle: Pet health data is too important to be owned by any single company. We're committing to transparent, ethical use of the data our community provides. This charter will be updated as we grow—and you'll have a voice in how.

This Document

This governance page is our public commitment. It's not legal boilerplate—it's a working charter we update regularly. Check back to see what's changed and why.

❓ Frequently Asked

Do you share data with vets?

Not without your permission. But if you want to share your CompanionCommons data with your vet, you can. We're working on integrations to make that easier.

What if I want to download my data?

You can export your pet's full history as a CSV file anytime. It's yours.

How long do you keep data?

As long as your account is active. If you delete your account, we delete all personal data within 30 days. Anonymized research data may be retained longer (consult our privacy policy).

Who's in charge of this?

CompanionCommons is a founding-member-first company. Major changes to this governance charter will require input from our community. We're building this together.

Is this GDPR/CCPA compliant?

Yes. We comply with GDPR (EU), CCPA (California), and similar regulations. You have the right to access, delete, and export your data anytime.
