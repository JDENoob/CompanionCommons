How it works

1

Join the Community

Begin Your Pet's Health Journey

  • Submit your companion's baseline information (yes, you can add multiple dogs!)
  • Receive a link and approve your submittal — this takes you directly to your dashboard
  • Upload your dog's favorite photo
  • Add any notes you want to remember
2

Share Quick Check-Ins

  • Receive a text letting you know it's time to check in
  • Click the link, which takes you right to your questions
  • Answer the questions and click submit
  • Takes 30 seconds!
  • Receive articles in your dashboard about breed-specific topics
3

See the Dashboard Journey

  • Add notes into your dashboard
  • Check on trends — see your companion's baseline vs. current check-ins
  • Download or print a summary to share with your vet or anyone in your pet's ecosystem

Week by week

How the Journey Unfolds

Day 1

Baseline

Submit your dog's starting scores across health categories.

Week 2 on

Weekly Check-Ins

Quick 30-second updates on mobility, energy, and appetite every week. Every 4th week also includes cognitive and weight. Breed-specific information chapters unlock as you go, starting week 2.

Week 12

Milestone

The original 12-week plan complete — a full picture built from real, consistent data.

Beyond Week 12

Keep the Story Going

Most owners keep logging well past this point — every additional check-in adds more information, sharpening your dog's journey and strengthening the bigger picture for pet health everywhere.
