What we help you track

Mobility & movement

Stiffness after rest, a slower pace on walks, hesitation with stairs or jumping.

Energy & appetite

Changes in activity level, enthusiasm for meals, or how much they're sleeping.

Cognitive & behavior

Confusion in familiar spaces, changes in sleep patterns, new anxiety or clinginess.

Changes worth noticing

After rest

Stiffness or hesitation when standing.

On walks

A slower pace, shorter distance, or different gait.

At home

Changes with stairs, jumping, sitting, or lying down.

Observations, not a diagnosis.
