Why This Movement Exists

I have spent my career solving complex operational challenges and learning one important lesson: You cannot improve what you do not understand.

As a pet parent, I've watched my companions age and wondered: How do I know if they're truly healthy? Are the small changes I'm seeing normal? Would knowing the collective voice of other pet parents help?

I've decided to build the largest community of pet parents driving the largest INDEPENDENT pet health initiative ever.

Our Mission

CompanionCommons exists because every pet deserves to be better understood.

Today, valuable pet health information is scattered across veterinary visits, products, supplements, and individual experiences. We believe pet families deserve an independent way to understand their companion's health journey over time.

Our mission is to create the most trusted source of pet health understanding built around transparency, community, and independence.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is CompanionCommons a pet product company?

No. CompanionCommons is an independent pet health intelligence platform.

Will my information be sold?

No. Your personal information will never be sold for marketing purposes.

How much time does this take?

Weekly check-ins are designed to take approximately 30 seconds. The initial profile setup takes approximately 2 minutes.

Do I need a senior dog?

No. We welcome dogs and cats of all ages as part of our community.

Because every pet has a story, and every health journey helps create a better understanding of pet health. Help build the community!
